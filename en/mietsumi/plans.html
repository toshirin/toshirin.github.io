Plans & Support

Free and Full Versions

Features included in the Free Version and added by the Full Version.

Available features

Free Version

  • 3×3×3 building area
  • White, Red, Blue, Yellow, and Green
  • Normal, See-through, and Wire display modes
  • Free rotation and zoom
  • Angle, Front, Left, Right, Back, and Top views
  • Total block count
  • Antenna display

The Free Version supports basic block problems and counting with the Antenna.

Full Version additions

  • 5×5×5 building area
  • 7×7×7 building area
  • Black, Gray, Purple, Cyan, Pink, and Orange
  • “Light” mode for light and shadow
  • Saving photos to the photo library

The Full Version is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. The price is shown in the app.

Purchase the Full Version

  1. Open “Settings.”
  2. Complete the parent check and open “Settings & Info.”
  3. Select “Unlock Full Version” and follow the App Store prompts.

Restore purchases

Select “Restore Purchases” under “Settings & Info” to restore a previous purchase. The Full Version can be used on supported devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
