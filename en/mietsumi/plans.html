Plans & Support

Free and Full Versions

Core tools for exploring blocks are free; larger creations, light, and photo saving are in the Full Version.

Compare versions

The Free Version includes the tools needed to build a 3×3×3 model and explore hidden blocks, counts, colors, and viewing directions.

The Full Version expands the space and color palette for creative building, and adds light and shadow controls and photo saving.

Free Version

  • 3×3×3 building area
  • White, Red, Blue, Yellow, and Green
  • Free rotation and zoom
  • Angle, Front, Left, Right, Back, and Top views
  • Normal, See-through, and Wire
  • Total count and Antenna

Use the core tools to examine hidden blocks, totals, and shapes from different directions.

Full Version additions

  • 5×5×5 and 7×7×7 building areas
  • Black, Gray, Purple, Cyan, Pink, and Orange
  • Light source and shadow controls
  • Saving creations to the photo library

Build larger, more colorful creations, compare light and shadow, and save your work as an image.

Purchase the Full Version

The Full Version is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. The app displays the price for your App Store region.

  1. Open the settings button at the top right of the screen.
  2. Complete the parent check and open “Settings & Info.”
  3. Select “Unlock Full Version” and follow the App Store prompts.

Restore a purchase

If a previous purchase is not recognized, select “Restore Purchases” under “Settings & Info.” It can be restored on supported devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

Purchasing and restoring require an internet connection. Apple processes the payment and purchase status.
